Objective Questionnaire

Every start draws 40 questions fresh from a 200-question pool, shuffled at random. Each question carries up to 4 objectives: select the correct one, then press Next (or Finish on the last). Previous goes back and lets you change your mind. The moment a question is displayed its own 30s window opens; if it is exhausted before Next (or Finish), the run FAILS and must be retried with freshly shuffled questions. Correctness is scored at Finish — no hints during the run. The clock is wall-clock based: researching in another tab does NOT pause it.
